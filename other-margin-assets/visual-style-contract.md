# Visual Style Contract - The Other Margin

## Direction
Single direction: **newsprint documentary editorial**. All imagery reads like
plates pulled from a critical social-documentary journal printed on warm
newsprint.

## Visual medium
Photography only (documentary / photojournalism). No illustration, no 3D,
no collage.

## Overall tone
Somber, dignified, observant. Quiet tension. Never sensational, never
stock-photo cheerful.

## Color system
- Base: black ink + warm cream newsprint (duotone grade)
- One accent detail: vermilion red (thin, occasional: a saree border, a thread, a light)
- Prohibited: saturated multi-color, blue sky, neon, pastel, pure white backgrounds

## Lighting rules
Hard directional light, deep shadow, chiaroscuro. Window light or single
source. No studio softbox look.

## Material rules
Analog film grain, subtle halftone/print texture, matte paper feel.

## Composition rules
- Documentary camera language, natural moments, no posed smiling portraits
- Faces anonymous or turned away where identity is not the point
- Generous negative space where typography will sit (cover, section plates)
- Vertical 3:4 for portrait plates, horizontal 3:2 for scene plates

## Detail density
Medium. Clean silhouettes and clear subjects; busy texture only in
environmental shots.

## Image treatment (page integration)
CSS: slight contrast lift, sepia-consistent grading via duotone overlay,
2px ink rule frame, mono caption strip beneath. Images sit inside the
margin-grid like printed plates.

## Prohibited elements
1. Any text, lettering, captions, or watermarks inside the image
2. Colors outside the ink/cream/vermilion system
3. Recognizable public figures or celebrities
4. Sensational violence, gore, or distress exploitation
5. Mixed visual systems across the set
